SafePermit AI
A simple helper to draft a Permit-to-Work checklist and risk-control summary. Built for supervisors and HSE teams to review—not to replace site procedures or authorization.
Prototype v0.1 · Free demo
Safety notice: This demo creates a basic template from your inputs; it is not an AI risk assessment and does not issue or approve permits. A competent person must verify hazards, controls, isolation, gas testing, and site-specific requirements before work starts.
